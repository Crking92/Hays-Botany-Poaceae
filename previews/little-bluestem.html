Little Bluestem

Schizachyrium scoparium

Follow one flowering stem to the tips of its leafy branches. Then look closer at how the spikelets attach.

See the parts

01 · Spikelet pair
FIELD DIAGRAM · NOT TO SCALE

A spikelet is a small unit that contains grass flowers. Look along one flowering branch for a pair. The sessile member attaches directly to the axis. The pedicellate member sits on a short stalk.

Flora check · The directly attached spikelet is 6–8 mm long in the regional treatment. The stalked companion is described as shorter and narrower.

02 · Spicate raceme
FIELD DIAGRAM · NOT TO SCALE

A narrow, spike-like cluster of spikelets. Follow each upper leafy branch to its tip. The Flora gives a length of 2.5–5 cm.

03 · Ligule
FIELD DIAGRAM · NOT TO SCALE

The small structure on the inner side where the leaf blade meets the sheath. Ease the blade away from the stem to look inside the junction. The Flora describes a firm membrane 1–3 mm long.

Open the collar photo reference. Use it to find the junction. A clear view of the exposed membrane is still needed.
