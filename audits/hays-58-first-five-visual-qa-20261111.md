## Individual photograph review findings

### Melica nitens

**Evidence reviewed:** 12 photographs from 4 distinct iNaturalist Hays County observations: [282514813](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/282514813), [350867217](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/350867217), [290019815](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/290019815), [361695388](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/361695388).

**Observed in actual photographs:** Loose panicle branches with pendulous pale spikelets, leaf blades and tussock context; some hand-scaled flowering heads.

**Not established from those photographs:** Fertile-floret count and sterile-rudiment shape not resolved; glume inequality and inner ligule dimensions not measurable.

**Conclusion:** MORPHOLOGY_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED (source photo evidence: MODERATE_SUPPORT).

**Next diagnostic photograph:** High-resolution separated spikelet showing 2–3 fertile florets, terminal sterile rudiments, long ligule with scale.

**Reference:** Flora of North Central Texas Melica key pp 1284; USDA NRCS Threeflower Melic Plant Guide

### Bothriochloa longipaniculata

**Evidence reviewed:** 12 photographs from 3 distinct iNaturalist Hays County observations: [353902207](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353902207), [353721821](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353721821), [353603714](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353603714).

**Observed in actual photographs:** Silvery narrow feathery panicle and upright grass culms; basal grassy tuft shown. Multiple close flowering head views are present.

**Not established from those photographs:** No glume macro adequate to check narrow shiny green vs broad dull glumes; reliable panicle-length measurement unavailable; can't separate from B. torreyana on these views.

**Conclusion:** BOTHRIOCHLOA_COMPLEX_CONFIRMED_SPECIES_HOLD (source photo evidence: GENUS_GROUP_SUPPORT_ONLY).

**Next diagnostic photograph:** A scaled complete panicle and detached sessile first glume macro, ideally compared at same magnification with verified B. torreyana.

**Reference:** Flora of North Central Texas Bothriochloa complex pp 1246; USGS Louisiana Plant ID B. longipaniculata comparison

### Paspalum langei

**Evidence reviewed:** 12 photographs from 5 distinct iNaturalist Hays County observations: [393289733](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/393289733), [323535066](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/323535066), [221600231](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/221600231), [374141829](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/374141829), [355601663](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/355601663).

**Observed in actual photographs:** Several slender racemes on branched flowering stalks, paired rows of spikelets in macro view, strap-like basal leaves, wooded/rocky ground context in some observations.

**Not established from those photographs:** First-glume presence and dark brown glandular blotches on second glume/sterile lemma cannot be verified at this review scale; species identification unproven.

**Conclusion:** PASPALUM_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED (source photo evidence: GENUS_GROUP_SUPPORT_ONLY).

**Next diagnostic photograph:** Macro of several detached mature spikelets, ventral/dorsal faces, glume attachment and glandular markings under scale.

**Reference:** Flora of North Central Texas Paspalum key and account pp 1306–1307

### Coelorachis cylindrica

**Evidence reviewed:** 12 photographs from 6 distinct iNaturalist Hays County observations: [232106369](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/232106369), [224007596](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/224007596), [279943509](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/279943509), [54843842](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/54843842), [374594336](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/374594336), [166995826](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/166995826).

**Observed in actual photographs:** Very narrow pencil-like cylindrical inflorescence with obvious successive nodes/segment pattern and hand scale; erect to arching culms and natural grassy habitat in several independent observations.

**Not established from those photographs:** Sessile spikelet rachis-niche architecture, pitted glume and exact membrane ligule cannot be resolved in photographs; confirm with dissection before final species-ID signoff.

**Conclusion:** DISTINCTIVE_INFLORESCENCE_COMPATIBLE_MICRO_HOLD (source photo evidence: STRONG_GROSS_MORPHOLOGY_SUPPORT).

**Next diagnostic photograph:** Macro along rachis joint showing spikelet seated in niche, fertile sessile + sterile pedicellate pair, glume pit and size scale.

**Reference:** Flora of North Central Texas Coelorachis cylindrica account p 1258

### Bromus catharticus

**Evidence reviewed:** 12 photographs from 4 distinct iNaturalist Hays County observations: [379499753](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/379499753), [358472027](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/358472027), [366862129](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/366862129), [358017136](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/358017136).

**Observed in actual photographs:** Open to somewhat loose grass panicle with flattened-looking elongated hanging spikelets; green grassy stand and culm close-ups visible.

**Not established from those photographs:** Sharp keel, exact awn lengths, lemma teeth/veins and collar/ligule not resolved; cannot rule out lookalike Bromus from gross view alone.

**Conclusion:** BROMUS_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED (source photo evidence: MODERATE_SUPPORT).

**Next diagnostic photograph:** One spikelet in profile with mm scale, separable keeled glumes and lemmas, awn/apical teeth, leaf sheath hairs and ligule.

**Reference:** Flora of North Central Texas Bromus key and rescuegrass account pp 1251–1252
