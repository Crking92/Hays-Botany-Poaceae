### Controls / avoid claims

1. iNaturalist observer and photo authors can differ; copy **per-photo attribution**. The original five Hays observations (IDs `136755826,232038649,135964547,141035831,23580302`) yielded **17 photo files, all CC BY-NC**, despite one observation itself being licensed CC BY. Source logs have each image's exact separate attribution.
2. Current CC labels in API do not encode the specific version of a BY/BY-SA license; verify the exact full license from the original photo page. **CC0 needs no license attribution legally, but the photographer should still be credited scientifically.** BY and BY-SA require notices and SA conditions where applicable.
3. Rights and local nativity are separate. These are possible **species-labeled teaching reference photographs**, not 2026 verified Hays specimens and not formal scored-key admissions. County BONAP mapping deferred.
4. Deduplicate by URL AND actual SHA of downloaded original against the existing GitHub archive and previously published gallery. Never republish all images indiscriminately; choose useful, distinct roles. The 15 previously source-cleared Group B images covering 5 species remain a **higher-priority immediate publication batch**.
