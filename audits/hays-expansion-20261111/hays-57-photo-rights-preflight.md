## Required release gates

1. **Photo rights:** reread exact iNaturalist photo source/license at time of import; photograph-level license, creator and derivative/attribution duties must be recorded. No photos were copied into any site in this pass.
2. **Noncommercial:** Do not casually interpret CC BY-NC as authorization for every municipal or monetized context. Confirm the specific project's use first.
3. **Photo role:** link only images that actually show the claimed ligule, awn, glume, leaf base, inflorescence or floral anatomy. A whole-plant photo cannot prove microscopic features.
4. **Botany:** photos with a usable license are not automatically correct species determinations or wild/established Hays County populations.
5. **Separate repository:** Continue existing 112-species Photo QA on main; this expansion rights inventory remains only on the `inat-coverage-audit-20261111` branch.
