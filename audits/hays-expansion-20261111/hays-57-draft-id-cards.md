## Import/merge conditions

1. **Accepted name & duplicates**: preserve current Kew treatment and full synonym map; old regional Flora names are for searching, not automatic extra species.
2. **Specimen ID**: use the saved linked observations and exact character request. Check glumes, lemmas, ligules, awns, perennial/annual habit and look-alikes on the same specimen; obtain expert or voucher corroboration where photo limits remain.
3. **Hays context**: determine whether an observed plant is naturalized or only planted turf, crop, ornament or forage. Captive=false on iNaturalist is not independent proof.
4. **Nativity and ecological use**: remain deferred to the later BONAP/NAPA plus county check; do not present introduced grasses as restoration plant recommendations.
5. **Photo rights**: save exact photo IDs, creators, CC license and attribution before any public web image copy; CC BY-NC-ND/all-rights-reserved are not blanket site permissions.
6. **Key traits**: only add known verified character states; empty means 'unknown', not absent. Run existing species and page QA after each safe merge.
