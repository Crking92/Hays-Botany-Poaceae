## B4-B — Lolium × boucheanum — public-domain exact-name leads HELD for taxon-concept ambiguity

**High-priority naming conflict: do not treat *Lolium × hybridum* Hausskn. as unconditionally equivalent to *L. × boucheanum* Kunth.** Current Kew 2026 recognizes **two accepted hybrid names with different formulae**:
- *L. × boucheanum* Kunth = *L. perenne* × *L. rigidum*: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:407425-1
- *L. × hybridum* Hausskn. = *L. multiflorum* × *L. perenne*: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:407468-1
- USDA GRIN specifically logs the **misapplied `L. × boucheanum auct.`** name under **`L. × hybridum`**, rather than simply synonymizing the typified species: https://npgsweb.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/taxon/taxonomydetail?id=426913
- Other floras including a French herbarium and older gallery *do use* `L. boucheanum` for the *multiflorum × perenne* forage hybrid. This is an **actual cross-author taxon-concept conflict, not just an interchangeable spelling**. Existing candidate record uses Kew's accepted *L. × boucheanum* and does not establish the photo concept. Photo owner/Chat 1 should flag this before incorporating hybrid teaching pictures under the wrong pedigree.

**Three original reuse-rights-cleared Flickr files, all explicitly titled `Lolium X boucheanum` but ID/taxon-concept HELD:**
