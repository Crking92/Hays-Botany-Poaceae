## Correct reference hierarchy and source-scope clarification

1. The user-supplied 1999 *Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas* provides direct species treatments when the modern species is actually named, and historical broader treatments must be marked as such.
2. The 2015 Hagenbuch & Lemke *Grasses of the Texas Hill Country* is an independent vegetative key with different coverage. Omissions from its 160-species appendix do NOT prove omission from the 1999 Flora. In particular, 1999 directly treats Phyllostachys aurea, Cortaderia selloana, Panicum miliaceum, Muhlenbergia capillaris and Miscanthus sinensis.
3. Modern Kew POWO/GrassBase, Flora of North America and regional specimen evidence are needed for taxon splits, current nomenclature and morphological disagreements.
4. Named hybrid problem: [Kew](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:407425-1) lists L. × boucheanum as perenne × rigidum. [EPPO](https://gd.eppo.int/taxon/LOLBO) uses that hybrid name for multiflorum × perenne. [USDA GRIN](https://npgsweb.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/taxon/taxonomydetail?id=100773) treats multiflorum × perenne as × hybridum and regards certain boucheanum applications as invalid/misapplied. Do not put a hybrid species filter online until name and specimen concept independently resolved.
