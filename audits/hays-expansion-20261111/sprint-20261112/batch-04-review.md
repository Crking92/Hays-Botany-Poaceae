## Strong new findings / source differences

1. **Pink tridens (*Tridens congestus*)** — UT Plant Resources Center [Williamson County account](https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Tridens%20congestus), directly screenshot-inspected, gives a diagnostic **short hard rhizome**, around **0.5-mm ciliate membranous ligule**, five to ten or more florets, a lemma tip **notch up to about 0.2 mm**, and a **0.3–0.5-mm midvein projection**. The staged Diggs 1999 packet calls the lemma apex "deeply cleft." These may reflect description scope or terminology; **do not impose a deeply-cleft binary filter before matching one ripe Hays lemma to both accounts**. Tuft and short rhizome coexist. [Kew accepts](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A257762-2) this Texas-native species, but no Hays BONAP county cell or actual Hays specimen was independently signed off.
2. **Crowfootgrass (*Dactyloctenium aegyptium*)** — Diggs staged packet 4 says typically **3–5 florets** per spikelet; UT's [detailed species description](https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Dactyloctenium%20aegyptium), directly screenshot-inspected, gives **(1–)2–3 florets**, often terminal sterile. Both sources describe a **spikelet-free branch tip projection** (UT ca. 2–4 mm, Diggs packet up to 7 mm). Floret counts may represent specimen or treatment differences; do not hardcode 4–5 florets or erase overlap. Also note UT's **hair-ciliate membranous ligule** around 0.3 mm: hairs vs membrane visual options can both be valid.
3. **Stapfochloa (*S. ciliata*)** — [Kew POWO](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A60469005-2/general-information) accepts *Stapfochloa ciliata* and places the historical *Chloris ciliata* as synonym. Historical Hays images show multiple curved digitate branches, but species-level verification demands a dissected **one fertile + two sterile floret** unit, ciliate lemma, and awn measurements matched to *S. canterae*. Texas nativity in Kew is not Hays County atlas verification.
4. **Hairawn muhly (*Muhlenbergia capillaris*)** — The staged packet 7 already corrected awn ranges and overlap with *M. reverchonii*. Typical pink ornamental photos cannot establish species ID, wild establishment or county nativity from the literature alone. **No minimum panicle length or simple 7-mm awn threshold** should be imposed on scored key until same-plant proof. [Kew](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A408959-1) confirms accepted name/native-Texas distribution.
5. **Janochloa antidotalis** — The combination was formally established **2023** ([IPNI](https://www.ipni.org/n/77319974-1)), and [Kew 2026](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A77319974-1/general-information) accepts it, treating *Panicum antidotale* as historical synonym. Kew places it **introduced in Texas**. Keep species-level accepted name and alias; do **not** substitute GBIF's older higher-rank fuzzy match. Historic Hays photo set is promising but lacks same-plant glume/floret/rhizome checks and forage-planting history.
6. **Dichanthelium acuminatum** — The [USFS taxon review](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/dicacu) and [NatureServe](https://explorer.natureserve.org/Taxon/ELEMENT_GLOBAL.2.155724/Dichanthelium_acuminatum_var_acuminatum) document broad vs narrow varietal species concepts. A single Hays shaded leafy patch without a mature spring spikelet cannot be signed off. Historical Panicum acuminatum variety hair states must not be applied indiscriminately to the current species.
7. **Eragrostis curtipedicellata and E. minor** — *E. curtipedicellata* has [viscid panicle axes](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A400988-1/general-information), but sheaths can lack exudate, so don't require sticky sheaths. *E. minor* has craterlike glands that vary by inspected organ; a sparse general-panicle photo cannot establish them. The saved two Sam Kieschnick CC BY source IDs remain metadata/preflight, **not** rights approval or newly viewed botanical proof.
8. **Nassella tenuissima** — [Kew](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A281184-2) accepts this taxon, native to Texas statewide, with *Stipa tenuissima* as historical synonym and a later *Jarava tenuissima* alternative combination. The FNA source and GrassBase differ slightly on ligule-tip shape. Saved Hays ornamentals and state nativity do not independently prove a local wild population or Hays native county status.
9. **Cenchrus ciliaris** — [Kew](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/395214-1) accepts this name over old *Pennisetum ciliare* and places it as **introduced in Texas**. The specific bur has bristles **joined at their bases** and dispersed with spikelet, unlike persistent free bristles of *Setaria*. This distinction must be photographed, not inferred from fuzzy cylindrical head.
10. **Cortaderia selloana** — The existing record for Hays observation **62141431** is explicitly ambiguous: several short cylindrical fountain-grass-like plumes do **not** convincingly show a broad branching pampas-grass panicle. Set it aside for expert identification rather than certifying it from the other five Hays labels. Check landscaped planting status and reproductive florets; the [Cal-IPC comparison](https://www.cal-ipc.org/resources/library/publications/ipcw/report35/) gives helpful distinguishing features but not a Hays observation signoff.
11. **Megathyrsus maximus, Melinis repens and Panicum miliaceum** — Literature explains the relevant names and plumelike/seedhead structures, but the historical source photos are either distant, flowering color alone, or potentially crop/seed-spill vegetation. Require anatomy and planting-origin context. [Kew Melinis](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A2945817-1/general-information) has the accepted old Rhynchelytrum synonym.
12. **Photo-first but identity-gated** — Five taxa in this batch have configured general reference images in `hays-candidates.json`; those photos do not independently determine identities of local Hays observations. The general-photo publisher retains manifest/rights ownership. This lane did **not** change the galleries.
