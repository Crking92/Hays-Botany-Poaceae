## Practical change discovered this pass

The botanical description of *Bothriochloa* vs *Dichanthium* is accurate but the photograph request must be **microscope-grade**. The central translucent/grooved pedicel feature cannot normally be resolved reliably in a phone panorama. [Faccenda's combined Hawaii grass key, published 2023, pp.99 ff.](https://hbs.bpbmwebdata.org/PUBS-ONLINE/pdf/op155p83-156.pdf) explicitly recommends a **20–40× dissecting microscope**, often with **dried raceme internodes/pedicels and both faces visible**. An intact ordinary flowerhead image without this groove is **not** evidence of a groove being absent. POWO corroborates the groove in *B. bladhii*, AusGrass confirms it is absent in *D. aristatum*. This publication is a **supplementary generic comparison**, not a replacement for our Hill Country/Diggs source hierarchy.

Also, **glume pits in *Bothriochloa bladhii* can be absent from some spikelets** ([Kew GrassBase](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:392701-1/general-information)); never require a pit on every sampled spikelet or reject the species solely because a head photo doesn't show one.
