## What the pressed plants really show

All three have rooted/basal plant material, broad linear leaves, and slender racemose inflorescence branches carrying closely arranged spikelets. The two 2021 images are especially good for flowering habit and abundance-related labels. The 2019 image also has mature racemes, but its yard setting requires an independent cultivation check.

**What cannot be proven at sheet resolution:** Individual lower glume lengths (FNA approximately 0.4–1.2 mm), uneven first-glume distribution on paired spikelets, and papillose-based short hairs on upper glumes and lower lemmas. These are the discriminating species characters. Also, TEX00568501 explicitly lists *Paspalum pubiflorum* growing alongside the candidate; compare that look-alike rather than assuming the annotation alone establishes the species.

## Source cross-check and next concrete step

Flora of North America describes *Paspalum langei* as a **perennial**, with 1–3(4) inflorescence branches and paired, imbricate spikelets 2.1–3.3 mm long. Confirm lower glume and pubescence microscopically on one of these original sheets or a new authenticated Hays voucher.
