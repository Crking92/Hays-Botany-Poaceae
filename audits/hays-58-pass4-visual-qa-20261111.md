# Hays County grass photo-ID QA — Pass 4 (18 taxa, 70 actual photos)

**18 Hays candidate name records inspected directly on image contact sheets.** These source media are individually CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA/CC BY-NC/CC BY-NC-SA licensed for temporary credited review, with original observation and photo IDs retained; no images committed to GitHub or published.

- Cumulative visual QA: **43 / 58 candidate names**, **289 actual photographs**.
- Remaining unreviewed candidate names: **15**.
- No grass added to the existing 112-card live ID Lab; no final botanist-species signoffs.

## Important findings

**Elymus glabriflorus:** Photographed awned spikes could belong to existing Elymus virginicus; nomenclature already resolves glabriflorus to virginicus. No separate card under current treatment, regardless of Hays iNaturalist label.

**Tridentopsis elongata:** Strong macro photos of narrow panicle, spikelet, collar and habit. Requires direct lemma/floret comparison with *T. mutica*, *T. buckleyana*. Suitable priority for targeted microphotography rather than another general habit picture.

**Aegilops cylindrica:** Jointed spike and dispersing spikelets well shown; scale/mature glume detail remains needed.

**Stapfochloa ciliata and Tridens congestus:** Distinctive flowering branches photographed with hand/metric scales; still need small species-specific diagnostic characters.

**Poor photo coverage:** *Dichanthelium acuminatum* and *Eragrostis curtipedicellata* each had only one licensed photo; *Muhlenbergia capillaris* only one ornamental-looking view. These should not be considered visually verified local taxa.

### *Tridentopsis elongata* (10 images)

Original Hays records: [366639842](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/366639842), [367070671](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/367070671), [377001623](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/377001623).

**Visible:** Exceptionally useful Hays photos: small tuft, long narrow panicles, scaly overlapping spikelets, partial collar/ligule, mature branches in hand.

**Not proven:** Species boundary versus Tridentopsis mutica and T. buckleyana depends on lemma/callus, florets, ligule and anther dimensions not all reliably measured.

**Status:** STRONG_TAXON_GROUP_PHOTO_SUPPORT_SPECIES_HOLD

**Next target:** Document dissected lemma apex/floret and mm-scaled ligule, compare the regional Flora's older Tridens muticus var. elongatus.

### *Bromus tectorum* (2 images)

**Visible:** Two observations display open, drooping, long-awned brome panicles.
