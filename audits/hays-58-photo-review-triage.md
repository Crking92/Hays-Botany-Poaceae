## Review sequence

1. Start with species having several independent observers and multi-photo observation sets.
2. Review the single-record and taxonomically disputed species more cautiously.
3. Check flora diagnostic morphology on actual observation images; label insufficient characters explicitly.
4. Flag planted ornamentals, forage crops, turf and uncertain geographic/identification contexts.
5. Do not mark any species merge-ready until actual visual QA and source acceptance is documented.
