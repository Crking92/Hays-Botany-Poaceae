## Detailed next actions

### Alopecurus carolinianus

**Visible-source license:** CC BY-NC-ND (1 photo). **Existing description:** Annual 7–35 cm tall; ligule membrane 3.5–7 mm; narrow head 2–6 cm long and 4–6 mm thick; silky one-flowered spikelets; lemma awn 3–5 mm from near base.

**Compare with:** Other Alopecurus and narrow seedheaded grasses. **Next:** Permission to examine source photo directly or a separate authorized specimen; macro of head, spikelet and ligule.

**Morphological reference:** Diggs, Lipscomb & O’Kennon, Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas (1999), user-provided Poaceae PDF. [Source](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/alopecurus/carolinianus/)

### Cenchrus setaceus

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (1 photo). **Existing description:** Tufted perennial, ligule of hairs, linear cylindrical plumose panicle; individual spikelets approximately 5–7 mm; bristles with plumose hairs.

**Compare with:** Cenchrus longisetus, Cenchrus alopecuroides and other ornamental Cenchrus. **Next:** Review original photo with permission or inspect a fresh specimen; check planting and escape context.

**Morphological reference:** Kew POWO / GrassBase account under Pennisetum setaceum. [Source](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A77106046-1/general-information)

### Digitaria californica

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (1 photo). **Existing description:** Perennial 50–100 cm tall; digitate or nearly digitate densely flowered branches; second glume and lower-floret margins bearing silky hairs 2–4 mm long.

**Compare with:** Other Digitaria, especially non-silky digitate species. **Next:** Inspect iNaturalist record directly if permitted, or obtain scaled spikelet-hair macro and full rooted plant; not automatic from two ID votes.

**Morphological reference:** Diggs, Lipscomb & O’Kennon, Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas (1999), user-provided Poaceae PDF.

### Elymus macgregorii

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (12 photos). **Existing description:** Compared with E. virginicus, glume awns 10–25 mm (vs about 3–10 mm) and spikelets loosely ascending. Early wildrye spikes have about 9–18 nodes separated 4–7 mm; auricles 2–3 mm.

**Compare with:** Existing Elymus virginicus, Elymus glabriflorus (alias under Kew), Elymus curvatus. **Next:** First choice for observer permission due to 12 source photographs; request scale-based glume and spike measurements and habitat context.

**Morphological reference:** Go Botany Flora Novae Angliae key + North Carolina vascular flora (post-1999 split). [Source](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/elymus/macgregorii/) [Source](https://auth1.dpr.ncparks.gov/flora/species_account.php?nrid_sciName=Elymus+macgregorii)

### Lolium × boucheanum

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (2 photos). **Existing description:** Accepted hybrid name; phenotype may overlap closely with ryegrasses; photo of leaf and spike alone need not demonstrate hybrid ancestry.

**Compare with:** Lolium perenne, Lolium multiflorum, Lolium rigidum. **Next:** Inspect original two images only with permission; keep hybrid-status hold unless exceptional diagnostic or provenance evidence.

**Morphological reference:** Kew POWO accepted hybrid name + project's nomenclature crosswalk. [Source](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A407425-1)

### Panicum dichotomiflorum

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (2 photos). **Existing description:** Coarse annual culms 1–2 m, erect to trailing; ligule 0.5–1 mm ciliate membrane with hairs about 2 mm; blades usually glabrous; late-season flowering.

**Compare with:** Panicum capillare, other Panicum with open panicles. **Next:** Permission to inspect source image or separate voucher; macro of ligule and mature floret plus habitat.

**Morphological reference:** Diggs, Lipscomb & O’Kennon, Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas (1999), user-provided Poaceae PDF.

### Sporobolus cryptandrus

**Visible-source license:** Unspecified / all rights reserved (3 photos). **Existing description:** Perennial with 2–4 mm long white hairs tufted at leaf sheath top; panicles often partly enclosed by upper sheath, commonly 15–30 cm long.

**Compare with:** Other Sporobolus, especially grasses with enclosed flowering panicles. **Next:** Direct image permission or fresh Hays specimen with upper sheath, ligule hairs and full seedhead on same plant.

**Morphological reference:** Diggs, Lipscomb & O’Kennon, Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas (1999), user-provided Poaceae PDF.


## Policy

These are targeted diagnostic requests, **not claims that the plant in the unreviewed image actually shows these characters**. All seven stay blocked from merging until actual observation images or other trustworthy specimens are evaluated, and wild establishment/planting context is checked. The 1999 Flora does not cover *Elymus macgregorii* as a modern split; modern post-1999 comparison is essential.
