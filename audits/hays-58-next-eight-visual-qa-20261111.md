# Hays County grasses photo-ID visual review — batch 2

**8 more species groups examined; 80 actual credited source photos inspected. No photo bytes were copied into the public repository.**

These source photos were reviewed in a one-day-only CC-licensed, noncommercial educational contact-sheet artifact. All conclusions are about what is visible, not assumed from the iNaturalist name or community research grade.

- **Batch 2:** 8 taxa, 80 images
- **Cumulative:** 13 taxa, 140 actual photographs inspected across batch 1 and 2
- **Species-level identifications independently confirmed:** 0; many photos give useful genus/group morphology, but not the minute diagnostic structures
- **High-risk identification:** *Bromus brevis*, with morphologically diverse source observations, an ID disagreement, and Hays establishment uncertainty
- **Important comparisons:** *Lolium perenne* vs *L. multiflorum* (and their accepted hybrid); *Bothriochloa bladhii* vs *Dichanthium aristatum* and local bluestems

## Per-species results

### Eragrostis superba

**Inspected 12 actual photos** from Hays observations [394713386](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/394713386), [354818424](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/354818424), [292593292](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/292593292).

**Observed:** Tufted dry to green bunchgrass; rather large flattened light tan spikelets on branched stems, with some hand-scaled seedhead views.

**Cannot yet verify:** Detached florets, glume and lemma veins/hair characters not resolved; introduced planting versus established escape not confirmed.

**Status:** ERAGROSTIS_GROUP_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_HOLD

**Next photo/ID target:** Close scaled mature spikelet, broken florets/lemma nerves and plant basal crown; comparison with other Eragrostis.

### Lolium perenne

**Inspected 12 actual photos** from Hays observations [354772388](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/354772388), [354778320](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/354778320), [353728699](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353728699).

**Observed:** Unbranched ryegrass-like flowering spikes with spikelets edge-on along central rachis; a basal bunch and habitat views.

**Cannot yet verify:** Awn presence/absence, floret and glume dimensions and discrimination from L. multiflorum or their hybrid not confidently resolved.

**Status:** LOLIUM_COMPLEX_NOT_SPECIES_CONFIRMED

**Next photo/ID target:** Paired species views with mm-scale mature spikelets, awn length, basal auricle and glume floret measurements.

### Bothriochloa bladhii

**Inspected 12 actual photos** from Hays observations [196764282](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/196764282), [410398752](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/410398752), [190252764](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/190252764), [309005126](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/309005126), [331046168](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/331046168).

**Observed:** Several fingerlike burgundy-purple racemes and paired spikelets/awn-like projections; strong magnification at hand scale and an exposed collar area in one observation.

**Cannot yet verify:** Distinctive paired-spikelet glumes, exact lemma awns, node collar anatomy and distinction from Dichanthium or KR bluestem unresolved.

**Status:** BOTHRIOCHLOA_DICHANTHIUM_COMPLEX_PHOTO_HOLD

**Next photo/ID target:** Detached sessile/pedicelled spikelet pair with ruler, sheath/ligule and node-hair ring, matched against B. ischaemum and D. aristatum.

### Dichanthium aristatum

**Inspected 12 actual photos** from Hays observations [250706832](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/250706832), [196764344](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/196764344), [374898786](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/374898786).

**Observed:** Two to several reddish spreading racemes with awns, some extremely close flowering axis views, a ligule/collar view and bunchgrass basal shoots.

**Cannot yet verify:** The distinguishing pair of sessile and pedicellate spikelets, nerve and awn anatomy versus B. bladhii not measured; species confirmation pending.

**Status:** DICHANTHIUM_COMPLEX_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_HOLD

**Next photo/ID target:** Macro of spikelet pair and rachis nodes with mm-scale, compare directly to B. bladhii and D. sericeum.

### Lolium multiflorum

**Inspected 12 actual photos** from Hays observations [44466836](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/44466836), [44466742](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/44466742), [352048437](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/352048437), [22685855](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/22685855), [275049650](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/275049650), [209103445](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/209103445), [159552822](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/159552822).

**Observed:** Flattened spikelets alternating along an unbranched ryegrass rachis, some clearly awned in close-up; roadside or grassy habitats.

**Cannot yet verify:** Not all specimens' awns, glume characters or hybrid status clear; L. perenne / L. × boucheanum lookalikes possible.

**Status:** LOLIUM_COMPLEX_NOT_SPECIES_CONFIRMED

**Next photo/ID target:** Detailed spikelet glume/awn and basal auricle comparison to L. perenne and the hybrid.

### Bromus brevis

**Inspected 7 actual photos** from Hays observations [115104343](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/115104343), [276102450](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/276102450), [267702112](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/267702112), [207333673](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/207333673), [208777132](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/208777132), [353987691](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353987691).

**Observed:** Some long open or somewhat contracted brome-like panicles and elongated spikelets photographed in lawns and disturbed grassland.

**Cannot yet verify:** Photographs vary markedly in spikelet/panicle habit; diagnostic glume and lemma metrics not shown. One observation has recorded ID disagreement. Current global literature does not clearly support a naturally established Hays population.

**Status:** HIGH_RISK_HAYS_IDENTIFICATION_HOLD

**Next photo/ID target:** Expert determination and Hays voucher/herbarium accession; high-resolution glume/lemma/floret dissection; check against B. catharticus and other Texas Bromus.

### Oplismenus hirtellus

**Inspected 6 actual photos** from Hays observations [39802176](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/39802176), [23760110](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/23760110), [353967258](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353967258), [98791209](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/98791209).

**Observed:** Shaded groundcover with broad lanceolate leaf blades, sprawling stem habit and distinct long-bristled/hooked-looking spikelets in close-up.

**Cannot yet verify:** The precise floret/awn base and bristle count distinguishing Oplismenus species not fully resolved; no plant establishment context vetted.

**Status:** OPLISMENUS_COMPATIBLE_MICRO_HOLD

**Next photo/ID target:** Macro on same plant showing spikelet pair, awn/barbs, leaf ligule and crawling rooted nodes.

### Echinochloa colona

**Inspected 7 actual photos** from Hays observations [368040343](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/368040343), [354850191](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/354850191), [136025063](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/136025063), [223116008](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/223116008), [353177417](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353177417).

**Observed:** Small tufted/stolonlike grass and short branched, sometimes crowded panicle with small spikelets; one wet-soil observation.

**Cannot yet verify:** Panicle architecture and glume/lemma hair/awn details do not conclusively separate E. colona from other Echinochloa and lookalike Paniceae.

**Status:** ECHINOCHLOA_GROUP_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED

**Next photo/ID target:** Macro with scale of mature spikelet, glumes and sterile lemma; leaf collar showing absence of ligule, compare to E. crus-galli / E. walteri.
