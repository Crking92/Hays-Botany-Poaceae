## Genus Aristida L. — p.382
“Low to moderately tall annuals and perennials,” lacking rhizomes or stolons. Blades narrow, usually involute. Ligule a ring of hairs a minute ciliate membrane. Inflorescence an open or contracted panicle of usually large, one-flowered spikelets. Disarticulation above glumes. Glumes thin, lanceolate, with a strong central nerve and occasionally 2 lateral nerves. Lemma indurate, terete, 3-nerved, with a hard, sharp-pointed callus at base, tapering gradually or less frequently abruptly to an awn column bearing usually 3 stiff awns; lateral awns partly or totally reduced in a few species. In some African taxa awns are plumose and feathery. Caryopses long and slender, permanently enclosed by firm lemma. Gould describes about 200 species in warmer parts of world, mostly subtropics (historical assessment).

**Interpretation:** genus description is source evidence for genus, not a direct species-by-species score without checking the named treatment. “Ligule a ring of hairs or a minute ciliate membrane” must retain alternatives; do not require a long hairy fringe for every species. Narrow/involute is qualified “usually”.

## Key evidence — pp.382–385
The provided images show a **partially complete** hierarchical key (continued from p.385 onward). Keep each trait/branch conditional; do not mark every species in a branch as having all ancestor states without validating key completeness, taxon names and historical concepts.

p.382 first fork: lateral awns greatly reduced, one-third or less as long as central awn and often rudimentary, versus other awn states. With reduced lateral awns: central awn with semicircular bend or spiral coil at base and annual plants proceeds to *A. ramosissima* (p.383; lemma 13–25 mm, central awn 15–25 mm); lemmas mostly 4–6 mm and central awn 5–9 mm to *A. dichotoma*. Other branch with central awn bend/spiral coil and perennial: awn column straight/slightly twisted to *A. ternipes*, strongly twisted to *A. erecta*.

p.383: lateral awns more than one-third as long as central awn: awns at least central with semicircular bend/spiral coil; awn column jointed at base and disarticulating at joint at maturity, lemmas 7–10 mm to base of awns to *A. desmantha*; awn column not jointed, persistent on mature floret, lemmas 4–7 mm to base of awns to *A. basiramea*. Another fork awns without semicircular bend/spiral coil. Lemmas >=16 mm to base of awns, annual to *A. oligantha*. Lemmas <=15 mm, annuals and perennials: at least lower sheaths lanate pubescent to *A. lanosa*; sheaths not lanate pubescent continue into panicle open/loose or contracted. Open panicles with stiff branches widely spreading and awns <=2.5 cm long to *A. barbata*. Further p.384 open-panicle paths include *A. pansa*, *A. divaricata*, *A. hamulosa*, *A. elata*, *A. purpurea*, *A. roemeriana*. The contracted-panicle paths include *A. arizonica*, *A. glauca*, *A. longiseta*, *A. longespica* (including var. *geniculata*), *A. adscensionis*, *A. affinis*, *A. purpurascens*, *A. fendleriana*, *A. cognata*. Additional notable exact-key measurements:
- *A. arizonica*: second glume mostly 13–18 mm (p.384); blades narrow but usually flat, often curled.
- *A. glauca*: second glume mostly 10–14 mm, as much as twice first; blades strongly involute (p.384).
- *A. longiseta*: awns 4–9 cm (p.384).
- *A. affinis*: awns 24–35 mm, lemmas 7–10 mm (p.385).
- *A. purpurascens*: awns 15–28 mm, lemmas 5–7 mm (p.385).
- *A. fendleriana*: first glume half to two-thirds as long as second; basal blades mostly 4–10 cm; panicles typically 5–10 cm excluding awns (p.385).
- *A. cognata*: leaves distributed on culms to well above base; basal blades mostly 10–25 cm; panicles typically 10–25 cm (p.385).
- *A. longespica*: annual with central awn mostly 5–15 mm and sharply reflexed on at least some spikelets; lateral awns one-third to slightly over half as long (p.385).
- *A. longespica* var. *geniculata*: central awn 15–(12-)36 mm as printed (range formatting should be checked against the image before normalizing), typically not reflexed; lateral awns two-thirds to three-fourths central (p.385).
- *A. adscensionis*: first glume distinctly shorter than second, panicles 6–15(–20) cm, relatively thick, lower branches 6–20 spikelets (p.385).

## Aristida oligantha Michx. — p.385, **incomplete treatment**
*Aristida oligantha* Michx., Fl. Bor. Amer. 1:41 (1803). Common name OLDFIELD THREEAWN. Gould figure 204 (not included in supplied images).
Visible species characters: tufted annual; culms wiry, glabrous, much branched at base and freely branched above base in age, 15–80 cm long. Sheaths rounded on back, glabrous or with a few hairs on either side of collar. Ligule a minute fringed membrane less than 0.5 mm long. Blades glabrous or sparsely hispid above, filiform, long-attenuate, involute tips; basal blades 10–25 cm long and 1–2 mm broad near base; upper blades short. Inflorescence few-flowered, purplish panicle or raceme mostly 5–14 cm long, with large widely spaced spikelets at branch tips and on short scabrous or pubescent pedicels. Glumes narrowly lanceolate, with subequal bodies [description continues on next page; no invented completion].
The genus key p.383 states lemmas 16 mm or more long to base of awns for this annual.
