## Most useful next targeted microscopy

**Best existing detailed source for future controlled verification:** *Stapfochloa canterae* has actual separated spikelets over ruler marks and a good hair-fringe collar. Compare fertile/sterile florets directly to *S. ciliata*; don't assume the species from overall head shape.

**Strongest Hays locality-independent evidence:** *Paspalum langei* has three authentic original TEX specimen sheets and 2021 conservation-land abundance labels, plus external spikelet closeups. It still needs lower-glume and papillose-hair examination, especially because *P. pubiflorum* occurs with it in one TEX collection.

**Best genus/group-level characters:** *Aegilops cylindrica* joined cylindrical spike; *Coelorachis cylindrica* jointed cylindrical raceme; *Pappophorum bicolor* many feathery awns; *Janochloa antidotalis* large swollen nodes and dark fertile spikelets. None of those gross traits by itself verifies all species-level floral details.

## Next species-proof images

- **Aegilops cylindrica:** Detach one whole internode + spikelet unit, show two glumes and florets with mm scale.
- **Tridentopsis elongata:** Flatten a mature second glume on contrasting background; illuminate the veins, measure to 0.5 mm, photograph blade width.
- **Echinochloa walteri:** Oblique macro of sheath hairs at bases, then detach a ripe spikelet beside millimeter rule.
- **Sphenopholis obtusata:** Isolate a spikelet showing the second glume face and blunt tip, plus scaled inner ligule.
- **Coelorachis cylindrica:** Remove one rachis joint and photograph pit and sessile/pedicellate pair at about 5×–10× with mm scale.
- **Paspalum langei:** Take 10×–20× photos of both spikelet faces from a verified Hays sample; isolate first glume and short papillose-based hairs; compare P. pubiflorum.
- **Tridens congestus:** Separate a mature lemma, photograph a split/cleft apex with a millimeter rule and compare Tridens flavus.
- **Pappophorum bicolor:** Dissect one fully ripe lemma; fan out all appendages under scope and count with 1-mm scale.
- **Janochloa antidotalis:** Photograph detached mature florets from this specimen, both glumes, and roots/rhizome system; reconcile with older Panicum antidotale key.
- **Stapfochloa canterae:** Measure fertile and sterile florets on the shown loose spikelets, and compare to independently verified S. ciliata at identical scale.
