### Priority problems identified

**Cortaderia selloana** — At least one credited Hays observation presents short cylindrical fountain-grass-like plumes rather than the large branched pampas-grass panicle expected. Suspect a mismatch or different ornamental. The wild-tagged status does not confirm escaped naturalized occurrence.

**Panicum miliaceum** — Source images show a rather narrow green immature-looking grain-bearing head instead of the characteristic mature loose proso-millet panicle. May be juvenile, cultivar-related or misidentified; needs a mature panicle and spikelet check.

**Stenotaphrum secundatum** — Photo set predominantly displays mowed turf. Useful documentation of local planting, not independently demonstrated natural occurrence.

**Echinochloa walteri** — Several images show promising hairy leaf sheaths and bristly, awned panicles in low/wet habitat, making this one of the stronger morphology-supported candidates, though not yet confirmed from full diagnostic spikelets.

## Species-by-species visible evidence and remaining steps

### *Phyllostachys aurea* — 7 photographs

**Original observations:** [329633572](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/329633572), [231317598](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/231317598).

**Visible:** Woody running bamboo thicket, narrow lanceolate leaves and node branching, multiple culms; compatible with Phyllostachys.

**Not established:** Basal culm internode crowding/compression characteristic of fishpole bamboo, grooved internodes and branch node details not demonstrated; planting or escaped-colony provenance not established.

**QA decision:** BAMBOO_GENUS_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_AND_ESTABLISHMENT_HOLD

**Required next images:** Low culm internodes and nodes with scale, stem groove, branching arrangement; check location context for cultivated colony.

### *Stenotaphrum secundatum* — 4 photographs

**Original observations:** [257665322](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/257665322), [40030898](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/40030898), [9341344](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/9341344), [481302](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/481302).

**Visible:** Broad-bladed mowed sod/turf habit, compatible with commonly planted St Augustine grass.

**Not established:** Turf scenes do not expose stolon joints, keeled compressed sheath, hair-ring ligule, or unique sunken florets on a corky rachis; research-grade wild tag does not establish spontaneous Hays occurrence.

**QA decision:** TURF_PHOTOS_SPECIES_NOT_CONFIRMED_WILD_UNVERIFIED

**Required next images:** Fertile inflorescence in situ; stolon nodes, compressed sheath, ligule; explicit planting/escape context.

### *Triticum aestivum* — 4 photographs

**Original observations:** [274743485](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/274743485), [275283904](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/275283904), [275283744](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/275283744), [276321132](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/276321132).

**Visible:** Distinct stiff, terminal wheatlike cereal spikes; some mature awned and some green heads, with full plant bases.

**Not established:** Wheat versus other Triticum, Hordeum or cereal cultivar forms cannot be established with certainty at this scale; observations may be crop or seed spill escapes rather than wild populations.

**QA decision:** CEREAL_WHEAT_LIKE_ID_CONTEXT_HOLD

**Required next images:** Individual sessile spikelets on zigzag axis, broad glumes, paired auricles, crop/volunteer site context.

### *Paspalum notatum* — 8 photographs

**Original observations:** [141259657](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/141259657), [8441988](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/8441988), [376894248](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/376894248), [192677193](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/192677193), [192676981](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/192676981), [192676809](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/192676809).

**Visible:** Clearly forked Y/V-shaped pair of spike-like racemes with small spikelets; smooth narrow leaves and mowed lawn/field context.

**Not established:** Paired racemes strongly support Bahia grass but microscopic broadly ovate glumes and seed size not confirmed; many records from lawns and could be introduced turf.

**QA decision:** PASPALUM_NOTATUM_STRONG_HEAD_SUPPORT_SPECIES_FINAL_HOLD

**Required next images:** Spikelet scale plus glume, rhizome and leaf collar; planted pasture/turf versus adventive context.

### *Setaria viridis* — 11 photographs

**Original observations:** [252033465](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/252033465), [208777994](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/208777994), [326920046](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/326920046), [254005512](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/254005512), [273230417](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/273230417), [272725049](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/272725049).

**Visible:** Bottlebrush-like narrow fuzzy panicles, often green but some tan, and visible radiating bristles, clumped annual-looking plants.

**Not established:** Short-burst bristle arrangement and finely rugose fertile lemma are not resolved against Setaria pumila, S. parviflora and other foxtails; many closeup images of head but no spikelet dissections.

**QA decision:** SETARIA_GENUS_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_HOLD

**Required next images:** Scale bristles/spikelets, mature lemma surface, basal roots and ligule; side-by-side other Hays foxtails.

### *Cortaderia selloana* — 7 photographs

**Original observations:** [62141431](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/62141431), [34559895](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/34559895), [16070876](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/16070876).

**Visible:** Some tall, silvery plume-bearing ornamental clumps look consistent with pampas grass; narrow, strap-like leaves in tuft.

**Not established:** At least one observation (62141431) shows multiple short cylindrical fountain-grass-like plumes that are NOT clearly the large branched Cortaderia panicle, raising a possible mixed/misidentified iNaturalist set. Cultivated landscaping likely.

**QA decision:** MIXED_OR_POTENTIALLY_MISIDENTIFIED_OBSERVATIONS_HOLD

**Required next images:** Recheck observation 62141431 first; compare Cortaderia broad branched panicle to fountain grass Cenchrus/Pennisetum; flower structure, hairy ligule, planting context.

### *Echinochloa walteri* — 11 photographs

**Original observations:** [313351555](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/313351555), [304955914](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/304955914), [242978468](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/242978468), [182490335](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/182490335), [134323843](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/134323843).

**Visible:** Well-displayed branched awned inflorescences, broad leaf blades, markedly hairy/papillose sheaths in closeups, and wetland edge habitat; multiple independent observations.

**Not established:** Awn and papilla-based sheath hairs strongly favor Walter's barnyardgrass, but the critical lower floret lemma/awn origin, glume size and comparison with E. crus-galli are not fully measured.

**QA decision:** STRONG_MORPHOLOGICAL_SUPPORT_SPECIES_FINAL_HOLD

**Required next images:** MM-scale mature spikelets and papilla-based sheath hair macro, paired comparison with E. crus-galli; check any leaf-collar ligule absence.

### *Rostraria cristata* — 5 photographs

**Original observations:** [74683342](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/74683342), [275155567](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/275155567), [276345150](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/276345150).

**Visible:** Short cylindrical green bristly panicle with densely overlapping awned or pointed spikelets; small tufted grass.

**Not established:** Actual glume and lemma awn origins, floret counts and ligule unclear; compact spikelike heads resemble other small pooid grasses.

**QA decision:** ROSTRARIA_LIKE_PANICLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED

**Required next images:** Spikelet dissection and awned glumes, lemma teeth, ligule, comparison with Koeleria/other annual grasses.

### *Panicum miliaceum* — 4 photographs

**Original observations:** [117019222](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/117019222), [232929491](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/232929491).

**Visible:** Green grain-bearing inflorescence, coarse grass-like blades and stem closeups visible; a separate full plant photographed in disturbed ground.

**Not established:** Several views show unusually narrow/contracted green heads versus typically broad, loose proso millet panicle, which could reflect immaturity or incorrect ID; no ripe grain/spikelet morphology or cultivar origin verified.

**QA decision:** POTENTIAL_MISIDENTIFICATION_OR_IMMATURE_STAGE_HOLD

**Required next images:** Mature broadly branched proso-millet panicle, plump spikelets, papilla-hairy sheath and 1–3 mm ligule; cropping/seed-spill context.

### *Setaria leucopila* — 6 photographs

**Original observations:** [142578061](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/142578061), [61797989](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/61797989), [61798023](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/61798023), [353823803](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/353823803).

**Visible:** Very narrow cylindrical green flowering heads with short bristles, basal leafy tussocks and flowering culms with hand scale.

**Not established:** Bristle length only approximate, maturity and perennial rhizomatous/tufted base uncertain; fertile lemma fine rugosity/wrinkles unresolvable.

**QA decision:** SETARIA_COMPATIBLE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED

**Required next images:** Scale 4–15 mm bristles and spikelets, mature finely wrinkled lemma, basal crown with roots; compare S. parviflora and S. viridis.

### *Sphenopholis obtusata* — 9 photographs

**Original observations:** [118433196](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/118433196), [115453422](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/115453422), [118412090](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/118412090), [275820080](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/275820080).

**Visible:** Narrow erect densely flowered spikes/panicles, tan maturing flowers, green panicle heads, a close sheath/leaf junction and multiple independent field scenes.

**Not established:** Second glume wide obovate blunt apex, floret arrangement and distinction from Koeleria or other wedgescales cannot be proven from contact-size images.

**QA decision:** SPHENOPHOLIS_LIKE_STRONG_HEAD_SUPPORT_FINAL_HOLD

**Required next images:** Macro of second glume face/apex and lemma, 2–3 florets, membranous ligule 1.5–3 mm, compared with S. interrupta.

### *Steinchisma hians* — 3 photographs

**Original observations:** [82946399](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/82946399), [82930069](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/82930069), [387883882](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/387883882).

**Visible:** Loose branched panicle with small drooping florets over lanceolate leaf blades, some contextual rocky ground photographs.

**Not established:** Only three images and no spikelet closeup, lemma/membrane characters or base; no independent confirmation versus related Panicum/Steinchisma.

**QA decision:** SPARSE_PHOTO_EVIDENCE_SPECIES_UNCONFIRMED

**Required next images:** Full flowering panicle under scale, mature spikelet with glumes/lemmas and ligule; verify older Flora Panicum hians combination.
