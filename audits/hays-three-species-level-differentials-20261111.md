# Hays County grasses — 3 priority look-alike differentials

**November 11, 2026. Three high-priority names were rechecked against species-level primary or authoritative taxonomic keys and the actual Hays source photographs.** This is new **diagnostic evidence**, not just more observation counts. No new cards merged.

## 1. *Stapfochloa canterae* versus *S. ciliata*

A 2015 systematic key to *Chloris* (the historical genus) gives two especially useful measurements: *Chloris canterae* has **fewer than 12 spikelets per centimeter** of flowering branch and a sterile-lemma awn **1.4–3.6 mm**. *C. ciliata* has **12–20 spikelets/cm** and sterile-lemma awn **0.4–1.6 mm**. Source: [Identification key, *El género Chloris en México*](https://abm.ojs.inecol.mx/index.php/abm/article/download/1091/1273?inline=1).

Review of Hays [observation 137935400](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/137935400) and specifically [photo 235666609](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/235666609) shows a well-scaled flowering branch. A visual spot count on one mid-branch segment suggests roughly **6–10 apparent spikelets/cm**, tentatively within the *canterae* range. This is **not a reproducible microscope count**: some spikelets overlap and rachis curvature/perspective matter. [Photo 235666602](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/235666602) shows detached florets with millimeter ruler marks, but fertile versus sterile lemma identity and the precise awn origin are not completely resolved.

**Result:** Upgrade evidence to `MORPHOLOGICAL_DIFFERENTIAL_SUPPORT_UPGRADED_NOT_FINAL_SPECIES_ID`. The two source photos offer testable, local, measured characters rather than habit alone. **Final action:** count 2–3 flattened raceme cm segments, separate an upper sterile lemma and measure the actual awn, and compare to verified *S. ciliata*.

## 2. *Paspalum langei* versus *P. pubiflorum*

The [Flora of North America *P. langei* account](https://biorepo.neonscience.org/portal/taxa/index.php?clid=8707&taxauthid=1&tid=20916) describes a **lower glume 0.4–1.2(1.8) mm** and short **papillose-based hairs** on the upper glume/lower lemma, with 2.1–3.3 mm mature spikelets. The [FNA *P. pubiflorum* account](https://biorepo.neonscience.org/portal/taxa/index.php?clid=8707&taxauthid=1&tid=20905) reports **lower glumes absent**, hairs glabrous or below 0.1 mm, and mature spikelets 2.8–3.6 mm. Length ranges overlap: **do not rely on length alone**.

Three actual Hays TEX specimens were opened and their labels read; Nov 12 2021 floodplain specimen TEX00568501 reports co-occurring *P. pubiflorum*. These real original specimens strongly document what collectors were calling *P. langei* in Hays but their sheet images do not prove the microscopic character. Next: isolate several spikelets (ideally same specimen) and inspect both glume and lemma faces at 10–20×.

**Taxonomic caution:** a published 2023 Hawaii account describes *P. langei* as annual, whereas Kew and FNA describe it as perennial. Neither annual-versus-perennial nor photograph-only habitat should be our key character until reconciled.

## 3. *Tridentopsis elongata* versus *T. mutica*

**Species-concept difference:** [Kew POWO](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A77313940-1) accepts *T. elongata* separately (2022 combination); [USDA GRIN](https://npgsweb.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/taxon/taxonomydetail?id=478322) recognizes *T. mutica* var. *elongata*; [Flora of North America](https://biorepo.neonscience.org/portal/taxa/index.php?clid=8683&taxauthid=1&taxon=12504) includes these characters under *T. mutica*. Keep *T. elongata* as the **staged accepted name under Kew**, preserving varietal and older synonyms.

The older variety-level differential is approximately **upper glumes 5.5–10 mm with 3–7 veins** for elongata versus **upper glumes 4–6 mm mostly one-veined** for mutica. The actual Hays observation photographs show mature glumes but **no isolated upper glume with countable nervation**.

**Result:** Separate taxon under Kew is supported by current taxonomy; original Hays observation ID remains **unverified at that rank**. Two previously returned *Tridens muticus var. muticus* TEX specimens are specifically **excluded** from elongata evidence.
