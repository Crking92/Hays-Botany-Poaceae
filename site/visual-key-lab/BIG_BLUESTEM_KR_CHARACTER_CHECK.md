## Result

Big bluestem remains unresolved under the full KR selection. Some variety forms have short or absent sessile awns and well-developed rhizomes; a broad claim that the species must have a long awn or short rhizomes would wrongly exclude them. The precise KR 1–1.5 mm awn, bent-and-twisted combination, papilla-based blade hairs and exact sessile/pedicelled glume features are not assigned without corresponding evidence. Absence of these descriptions is not proof that a trait is absent.

The useful source comparison is sessile spikelets 7–11 mm versus KR Gould 3–4.5 mm, concave two-keeled FIRST glume, equally long single-keeled SECOND glume, thin mostly hyaline lower lemma, deeply two-lobed upper fertile lemma with an awn between its lobes, and flowering-rachis node hair tufts. No new measured-spikelet-size filter is invented; measurements are field-check context. A single keel is not one vein, a flowering rachis node is not a culm node, and twisting alone does not establish a bent/kneed awn.

## Scoped varieties and second-book cross-check

Gould's historical var. chrysocomus has copious usually golden/yellow 3–5 mm rachis-internode hairs and developed rhizomes. Var. gerardii has mostly 1–2 mm sparse-to-copious hairs, absent/short rhizomes and 8–20 mm sessile awns. Var. paucipilus has variable typically short sparse hairs, developed rhizomes and awnless or short-awned sessile spikelets, rarely exceeding 5 mm. These are source alternatives, not a required combination on one plant. No 2-mm lower endpoint or exact 1–1.5 mm awn interval is invented for the short-awn variety.

Directly read Diggs, Lipscomb & O'Kennon (1999), Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas, pp.1238–1239 through the owner's connected reference PDF. It corroborates the large sessile/awnless pedicelled pair and 2–7 digitate branches. Its subsp. gerardii has (7.5–)10–25 mm awns, short/absent rhizomes and 0.4–2.5 mm ligules; subsp. hallii has absent or up-to-8(–11) mm awns, developed creeping rhizomes and (0.9–)3–4.5 mm ligules. Diggs includes historical var. paucipilus under hallii and treats chrysocomus as a hybrid of the two subspecies. These concepts/ranges stay separate rather than being forced into Gould's three-variety scheme. The Diggs generic two-floret evidence remains explicitly generic, not relabeled as an exact species count.

Gould's unmeasured short stiff fringed ligule is not assigned a new numerical length. Its flat 5–10 mm blades supplement rather than erase H&L folded and 2–10 mm reports. Culm glabrousness is not transferred to a node-specific hairless choice. The page does not describe sheath surface, so no new Gould hairless-sheath state is manufactured.
