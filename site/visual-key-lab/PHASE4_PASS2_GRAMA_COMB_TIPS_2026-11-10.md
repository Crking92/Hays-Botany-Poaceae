## Corrected diagnostic wording from primary flora

The regional Bouteloua key (printed p. 1247) separates:

- **Blue grama**: generally 1–3(4) curved combs, with the branch axis **NOT continuing as a naked sharp point** beyond spikelets. The species account (printed p. 1248) specifically states **apical rudimentary spikelets absent**. We corrected an earlier source-backed checklist that wrongly suggested a terminal rudimentary spikelet. **Awns on florets are not rachis extensions.**
- **Hairy grama**: 1–4 comb-like branches with an obvious **naked rough/scabrous rachis tip** beyond normal spikelets (species description about 2.3–4 cm branch length including tip). The key distinguishes it from **tall grama (*B. pectinata*)** by lack of a tuft of hairs at the base of the lowermost rudimentary floret; B. pectinata has that tuft and a projecting axis-like rudimentary spikelet. Do NOT force a species decision from an unscaled silhouette alone.

The direct flora source is recorded inside each species' `character_evidence.head` and in the first `diagnostic_checks` row. The key has **not** been changed to claim the full gallery or ligule is certified.
