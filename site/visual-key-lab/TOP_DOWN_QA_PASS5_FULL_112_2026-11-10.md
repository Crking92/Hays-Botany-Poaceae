## Last 38: source-backed broad-grass checks

1. **Threeawns and wintergrass:** *Aristida purpurea* can have contracted OR open panicles; *A. oligantha* is an annual with three awns from the same floret. *Nassella leucotricha* may have an apparently absent or membranous ligule, and one 4.5–10 cm awn, not three.
2. **Short-spike and grain grasses:** *Cynodon dactylon* has a short ciliate MEMBRANE, plus stolons and rhizomes; *Eleusine indica* has flattened near-digitate spike-bearing branches; *Hordeum pusillum* has barley spikelet groups, not Setaria bristles; *Secale cereale* has awned two-flowered spikelets; Canada versus Virginia wildrye differ in overall spike posture and density but require real attached spikelets.
3. **Bromes, fescues, oats:** *Bromus japonicus* annual versus *B. pubescens* woodland perennial; *B. texensis* Texas native annual versus introduced *B. lanceolatus*, both still need micromorphological checks. *Festuca octoflora* has variable awn length; *F. versuta* confirmed as tufted perennial with short membranous ligule from *Grasses of the Texas Hill Country*. *Catapodium rigidum* is called *Desmazeria rigida* in the older flora.
4. **Distinct heads:** Added photo-based **tangled dark awns** selection for tanglehead (*Heteropogon contortus*) using an exact-species CC BY-SA reference. *Chasmanthium latifolium* has conspicuously flattened hanging spikelets; curlymesquite has short triads of spikelets on stolon-connected tufts; tobosa has a compact false spike with short underground rhizomes.
5. **Dropseeds and small grasses:** *Sporobolus vaginiflorus* has a tiny contracted partially sheathed flowering head; *S. clandestinus* has a longer spiciform panicle often emerging from an inflated sheath; *S. compositus* has a narrow, often partly enclosed panicle and minute ciliate membrane, but infraspecific taxonomy must not be blindly equated with modern segregates. *Munroa pulchella* has short stolons, a hair-fringe ligule and a very small, leaf-surrounded head.
6. **Lovegrass relatives and taxon crosswalks:** *Erioneuron pilosum*, *Tridens albescens/flavus/texanus*, *Tridentopsis buckleyana/mutica*, and *Triplasiella eragrostoides* received broad-source confirmation. For *Triplasiella*, Peterson et al. 2016 describe open panicles 10–30 × 8–20 cm and distinguish it from much smaller *Triplasis*; the accepted name is different from historic *Tridens eragrostoides*.
7. **Bluestem/grama/revision closure:** Source notes now distinguish the spreading combs of blue grama, branch-tip extension on hairy grama, the axis-like terminal rudimentary floret of tall grama, and short *pectinate* combs of red grama. **Do not mistake red grama's short pectinate branches for the non-pectinate short racemes of Texas grama.** Bushy *Anatherum glomeratum* differs from the dispersed upper flowering branches of *A. virginicum*. Kleberg bluestem's longer awns and bearded nodes distinguish it from KR bluestem.
8. **Airy panicle closure:** Pine muhly (*Muhlenbergia dubia*) has a firm 4–10 mm membranous ligule, narrow 10–40 cm panicle and variable 0–6 mm awns. *Dichanthelium commutatum* may have a minute hair ligule or none. Lindheimer rosette grass has a hair fringe in a USDA Forest Service treatment under *D. acuminatum var. lindheimeri*; this is **not automatic taxonomic equivalence**. *Eragrostis lugens* has an open loose panicle, flexuous branches, and a hair-fringe ligule.
