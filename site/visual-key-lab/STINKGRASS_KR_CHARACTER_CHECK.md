## Primary evidence and precise organ mapping

Gould describes a tufted annual 10–60 cm, branching at base, frequently geniculate, usually with a gland ring BELOW nodes. Sheaths overlap and are hairless except at a pilose throat, usually with gland pits on the keel and sometimes upper nerves. Blades are hairless, flat or sometimes folded, 2.5–7 mm wide, with gland pits on midrib or lower surface. Basal branches do not establish a runner; gland pits do not establish papilla-based hairs. Open sheath margins in H&L remain compatible with Gould's overlapping margins.

Panicle ovate/oblong, dense-flowered to open, with stalked spikelets; spikelets 6–20 by 2–4 mm and 12–40-flowered, rachilla persistent. Glume keels have gland pits. Membranous, closely overlapping, dull gray-green lemmas are 2.2–2.8 mm long, with conspicuous lateral nerves and usually glandular-pitted keels. Glands on different organs are separately selectable. Neither keel nor conspicuous nerves supplies an unstated vein count. Hyaline persistent paleas have ciliate keels; grain, chromosome, phenology and historical distribution remain non-filter context.

The page does NOT describe a ligule. Long hairs at the sheath throat do not prove the true ligule type or length. Existing H&L hair-fringe/length evidence is preserved unchanged rather than relabeled as Gould. Prior Kew conditional 1–3-vein glumes and broader 5–60-floret descriptions are retained alongside Gould; no unsupported one-vein assignment is made to both glumes from this page.
