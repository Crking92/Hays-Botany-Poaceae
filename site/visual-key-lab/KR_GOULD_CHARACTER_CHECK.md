# KR bluestem — Gould pp.602 and 604

The owner's photographs show the *Bothriochloa ischaemum* var. *songarica* treatment beginning on printed p.602 and continuing on p.604 of Frank W. Gould (1975), *The Grasses of Texas*. The intervening p.603 has figure captions. The continuation's first word completes the grazing-or-cutting condition. The separately numbered *B. pertusa* treatment below is not merged into KR. Copyrighted page images are not republished.

## Evidence and scope

`gould-kr-character-supplement.json` adds 14 positive-only advanced choices and source mappings across 13 existing groups. The original 1,815-row source master and its historical checks remain unchanged. The complete UI has 24 groups and 148 choices; the nine main tabs and seventeen promoted illustrated choices remain intact.

- Gould's historical variety is a tufted perennial with erect or decumbent culms. Some stoloniferous/rhizomatous growth is expressly conditional on close grazing or cutting. The new choices state that condition, retain H&L's competing no-stolon/no-rhizome report, and never infer either organ merely from a trailing stem.
- Shortly bearded culm nodes become glabrate with age. Hairless older nodes, hairless sheaths and papilla-based blade hairs near the ligule are separately supported.
- Linear-attenuate blades are mostly 2–4 mm wide and 4–20 cm long, with the top blade greatly reduced. The short truncate membrane is usually 1 mm or less; no fabricated lower measurement endpoint is added. The nearby *B. pertusa* ciliate-ligule wording is not assigned to KR.
- Slender flowering branches are terete below the spikelets; rachis internodes and pedicels have marginal cilia and a narrow medial groove at least on their terminal units. This groove is not a glandular glume pit.
- The sessile FIRST glume explicitly lacks a glandular pit/depression, is usually rough along its margins and stiff-hairy on its lower back, with an acute tip. The stalked FIRST glume may be hairless or hairy below midlength. Both organ scope and permitted variation are preserved.
- Gould prints a geniculate/twisted lemma awn mostly 1–1.5 mm. The main short-single awn path and the existing precise short-awn option now receive source support. The pedicelled staminate spikelet is awnless. The inherited FNA 9–17-mm report remains a source discrepancy, not silently converted units. Johnsongrass's deciduous awn is not transferred to KR.
- Height/culm length, branch number and length, panicle exsertion/length, sessile spikelet size, chromosome reports, historical distribution and phenology are retained as non-filter source context. The occasional >100-cm low culm is not misrepresented as an erect height.

Three Gould diagnostic checks are added to KR's Lab detail panel at runtime. All states remain positively scoped to historical var. songarica within the existing species aggregate, without asserting every variety, age or management condition has every trait. No new taxon, admission, county-nativity, photo, image credit, diagram or annotation changes.
