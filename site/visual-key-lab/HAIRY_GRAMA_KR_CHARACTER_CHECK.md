## Source/organ distinctions

Gould describes a short-lived tufted perennial, 15–40 cm, with hairless culm nodes and lower branching. Sheaths hairless or lowermost thinly pubescent, throat pilose; short ciliate membrane with no measured Gould ligule height. Blades 1–2 mm, flat or subinvolute, with sparse papilla-based hairs on lower margins. The 2-mm width boundary overlaps the lab's narrow choice; it cannot by itself exclude a KR narrow observation.

Each of 1–4 comb-like branches carries 20–50 spikelets. Branch rachis extends as a bare point 5–8 mm or more. Glumes differ: first 1.5–3 mm minutely hispid; second 3–5 mm with 1–2 mm papilla-based hairs on midnerve and a minute apical awn. Perfect-floret lemma 5–6 mm, more or less puberulent on back; palea about as long. There are TWO rudimentary florets, the lower with THREE rough awns about4 mm and upper a minute scale. Three awns are not three florets, nor automatically three fertile-lemma awns. The existing FNA fertile-awn record stays intact and separately attributed.

Gould says rachilla below lower rudiment NOT hairy. The prior broader FNA record allows glabrous/pubescent rachilla below second floret.
