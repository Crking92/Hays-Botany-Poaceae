## Primary mapping and context

Tufted perennial, firm base without runners, culms unbranched above base. Firm is not knotty. Lower sheaths, especially sterile-shoot sheaths, often flattened/sharply keeled; hairless sheaths or pilose lower sheaths. Blades hairless, rough or sparsely pilose, 2–6(–8) mm; inrolling often on drying, not a mandatory fresh-leaf state.
