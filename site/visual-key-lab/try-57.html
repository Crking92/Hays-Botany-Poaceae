How to interpret an experimental result

One observed character cannot identify a grass. A candidate with unscored characters survives more selections because those fields are unknown, not because the grass matches every choice. Check the candidate’s source-described characters, species comparisons, flowering season, original photo credits and local origin before calling the identification.
