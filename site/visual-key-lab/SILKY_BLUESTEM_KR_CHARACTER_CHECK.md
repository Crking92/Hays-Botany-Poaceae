## Structural source path

Gould describes a tufted erect perennial mostly50–100 cm. Dense silvery spreading hairs beard the culm nodes; these are not the true ligule, an erose1–2 mm membrane. Leaves commonly glabrous or nearly so, but infrequently sheath and both blade faces villous with spreading hairs; common/rare conditions remain eligible. No blade papilla bases are inferred from glume papilla-based hairs. Blades mostly2–5 mm; the5-mm boundary overlaps medium without claiming a general5–10 mm width.

The2–7 conspicuously villous branches,2.5–6 cm, cluster densely and erect at the apex. Rachis/pedicels narrow, densely villous, slightly flattened. General villosity is not automatically marginal cilia or proof of a KR medial groove. Sessile spikelets2.5–3.5 mm, paired with similar-sized awnless pedicelled companions; dark sessile lemma awns twist and are twice geniculate.

The most precise check is the outer-glume pattern: sessile outer glume thin rounded apex delimited by a line of long usually papilla-based hairs, continuing down both sides of dorsal surface. The pedicelled outer glume often has a hair line across tip/down sides. Exact outer-glume position is retained rather than inventing a first-glume gland pit/nerve count or a lemma-hair state. A generic white node beard or hairy head alone is insufficient.
