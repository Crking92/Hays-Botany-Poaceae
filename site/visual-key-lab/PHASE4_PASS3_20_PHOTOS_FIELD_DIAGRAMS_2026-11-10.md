## Specific quality corrections

1. Blue grama original photo `i=1` is **a detached close-up of floral/spikelet structures**, not a whole mature eyebrow comb. The photo is valuable but its previous gallery caption promoted the wrong role. Caption corrected; image retained.
2. Blue grama original photo `i=2` **visibly shows hairs around a blade/sheath junction**, but the true inner ligule edge cannot be confidently typed or measured. Preserve the uncertainty.
3. Hairy grama original photo `i=3` resolves hairy/bristly comb surfaces and awns, but **does not isolate the second glume**; revised caption and keep isolated-glume target open.
4. Tall grama Uvalde comb-tip photo `i=1` is a useful comparison, but **does not prove the diagnostic lowermost rudimentary-floret basal hair tuft** against hairy grama.
5. Little bluestem `i=3` is a field stem/old sheath reference and cannot show the detailed flowering spikelets. The `i=0` and `i=6` photos better handle spikelet pair and hairy raceme teaching roles.
6. Silky and Kleberg node photos (`i=0`) show a **culm NODE hair ring**, **not the true ligule**. Silky `i=4` actually shows a thin ligular membrane at the opened collar; this is the accurate ligule teaching example.
7. The previously shared **flat oat-like / loose awned oat-like** mini drawing was split into two original patterns (broad pendent Chasmanthium-style spikelets versus more open, pointed, awned Bromus-style spikelets); explanatory schematics remain subordinate to licensed field photos.

## Photo and morphology gaps remain open

Little bluestem: inner collar, paired dissected spikelets and whole dormant tuft. Silky: four remaining existing photos plus same-voucher comparison of sessile/pedicelled spikelets. Kleberg: four remaining photos and opened inner ligule.
