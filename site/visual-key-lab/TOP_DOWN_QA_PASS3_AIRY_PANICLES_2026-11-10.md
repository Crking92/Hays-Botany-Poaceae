## Source-backed group changes

| Species | Changes / audit | Evidence and caution |
|---|---|---|
| *Panicum hallii* | Hair-fringe **and** membrane broad options allowed | Flora p.222 describes a short fibrous splitting membrane with marginal hairs. A hair fringe should not erase the underlying membrane |
| *Panicum capillare* | Hair fringe **and** membrane alternatives retained | Regional flora p.220 says hairs; existing Hays card citing FNA notes a short ciliate membrane. **Source interpretations need a measured collar photo** |
| *Eragrostis spectabilis* | Bunch and knotty-rhizome options | Flora p.196 documents a knotty rhizomatous base. The current photos do **not** expose the underground structure; don't claim a photographed rhizome |
| *Eragrostis secundiflora* | Hairy margin and membrane broad options | Regional subsp. oxylepis has a very short membranous base hidden by long mouth hairs (flora p.196); do not impose this subspecies state on every taxon without verifying |
| *Muhlenbergia reverchonii* | Short/inconspicuous and potentially visible longer awn alternatives | Flora p.212: lemma awn 0.5–4(–7) mm; broader photo choice cannot depend on a fixed long/short dichotomy |
| *Muhlenbergia schreberi* | Short/inconspicuous and possibly visible awn alternatives | Flora p.214: awn 1.5–5 mm; lower reclining stems can root at nodes. No root-node photo was treated as verified |
| *Muhlenbergia ×involuta* | Membranous ligule; **no conspicuous long** awn | Flora pp.210,212: awn 0.5–2 mm from between minute teeth; historical hybrid *M. lindheimeri × M. reverchonii*, which can't be proven by a generic open-panicle photograph |
| *Muhlenbergia paniculata* | Existing open, wiry, branching state checked | Flora p.240 under historical name *Schedonnardus paniculatus*: sparse radiating branches along a loosely coiled axis |
| *Dichanthelium pedicellatum* | Existing true hair-fringe ligule checked | Flora pp.224,226 under *Panicum pedicellatum*: a usually 0.3–1 mm hair fringe, not exterior blade-margin hairs |
