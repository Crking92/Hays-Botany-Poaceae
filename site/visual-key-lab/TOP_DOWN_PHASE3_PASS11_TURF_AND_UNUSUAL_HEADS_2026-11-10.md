## Field differences now in the species detail panels

| Species | Main newly documented anatomical structures | Photo and taxon caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Buffalograss — *Bouteloua dactyloides* | Usually **dioecious**, occasionally monoecious: MALE heads with 1–4 comb-like branches, FEMALE heads with short hard non-spiny, toothed, bur-LIKE seed clusters of normally 2–5 one-flowered spikelets; short **0.5–1 mm ciliate membrane** inner ligule; aboveground rooted stolons | Broad `head` states now **combs and burrs**. Female seed clusters are formed from hardened glumes / branch axis, **NOT** the fused spiny bristles of Cenchrus; do not call separated male/female plants zones of one gamagrass flowerhead. Each sex photo must be authenticated |
| Bermudagrass — *Cynodon dactylon* | Often **4–6** terminal one-sided fingers with 2–3 mm one-FLORET spikelets, plus BOTH aboveground stolons and underground slender scaly rhizomes; true ligule short ciliate membrane ~0.2–0.5 mm, which FNA also calls a hair fringe; longer throat hairs are separate | Compare goosegrass 5–7-flowered spikelets and pale compressed crown, and buffalograss male/female reproductive difference. Existing broad `hairs/membrane` tolerance preserved |
| Vine mesquite — *Hopia obtusa* (old *Panicum obtusum*) | Long white-stemmed stolons with swollen hairy rooting nodes; a contracted upright panicle with **1–4 very short appressed one-sided branches**; smooth **3.4–4 mm OVAL, blunt** spikelets with one basal male and one fertile upper floret; almost full-length **5-veined lower / 7-veined upper glume**, true 1–2 mm eciliate membrane ligule | The head is not a bare unbranched spike. To prove the anatomy we need an attached complete branch and separated glumes; don't count historical name as another species |
| Hairy woollygrass — *Erioneuron pilosum* | Low tuft with folded white-cartilaginous-edge gray-green basal leaves; short **~2–5 cm dense clubby head** above nearly leafless culm; **7–18 florets** per ~12–19 mm hairy spikelet; three-veined, hairy 4.5–6 mm lemmas each with one **1–2 mm awn** from between tip lobes; hair fringe ligule ~0.5 mm | Contrast Munroa, whose flowering packets are enclosed in short leafy fascicles, not on naked head stalk; color alone not diagnostic |
| Fluffgrass — *Munroa pulchella* (older *Dasyochloa / Erioneuron pulchellum*) | Dwarf short-stoloned perennial with 1–5 separate 7–13 mm spikelets in **~1–2 cm flowering packets embraced by pointed leaf fascicles**; typically 6–12 florets, persistent ~6–8 mm one-veined glumes and 3–5 mm deeply 2-lobed lemmas with ONE **1.5–2.5 mm** awn | **Source conflict on ligule length:** Kew GrassBase ~0.5 mm hair fringe while FNA under an older combination gives 3–5 mm. Do not use a fixed ligule-length cutoff without local specimen and accepted-name crosswalk. Existing broad hair ligule preserved |
| Ozarkgrass — *Limnodea arkansana* | Annual narrow noncylindrical panicle; true **1–2 mm lacerate-ciliate membrane**; one fertile floret per 3.5–4 mm laterally flattened spikelet, stiff **3–5-veined glumes**, a sterile rachilla extension; three-veined lemma with TWO tiny terminal teeth and ONE dorsally arising 8–11(12) mm bent/twisted awn | Do not mistake one long awn for threeawn genus Aristida, or tiny barren rachilla extension for a developed second floret. Rooted annual voucher and close lemma are pending |

## Photo roles to check after current legal/identity audit

- Buffalograss: **separately authenticated male comb** and **female hardened non-spiny cluster**, with an opened female floral branch and exact spikelet counts; real 0.5–1 mm ciliate membrane.
- Bermudagrass: one single-flowered sessile spikelet on a digitate ray, connected rooted stolon and scaly buried rhizome, short true ligule vs longer throat hairs.
- Vine mesquite: same-plant long stolon with hairy enlarged root node, complete short lateral branch of rounded spikelets, separately labeled 5-veined / 7-veined glume.
- Hairy woollygrass vs Munroa: **head above bare culm vs packet inside leafy fascicle**, floret count and individual lemma teeth plus small single awn. Compare inner collar under magnification to resolve Munroa source length discrepancy.
- Ozarkgrass: actual 3–5-veined glumes, sterile rachilla extension, and a single 8–11mm geniculate dorsal awn on a two-toothed lemma, with rooted annual and late-fall flowering head where visible.

Use the **existing** one shared Photo Needs Queue after actual approved-photo inspection; do not bulk-download or create another spreadsheet. County-native status remains a **separate BONAP NAPA** check, never inferred from FNA general ranges.
