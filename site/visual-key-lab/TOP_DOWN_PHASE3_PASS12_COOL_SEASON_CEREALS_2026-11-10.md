## Separating look-alikes under a hand lens

| Species | Published examination target | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| **Canada wildrye** | Usually **2** multi-floret spikelets per node on a sometimes nodding 6–30cm ear. Glume bases often form a **sharp V**; mature lemma awns long and divergent/curved. Membranous inner ligule and clasping auricles. | Virginia hybridizes and may overlap. Do not use nodding alone or a detached glume |
| **Virginia wildrye** | Usually paired multi-floret spikelets, stiffer erect 8–15cm ear sometimes in flag sheath. Glume bases bow as **U** and lemma awns relatively straight/shorter; ligule membrane and clasping auricles. | Use multiple mature nodes and real glume insertion; hybrid/intermediate records remain *Elymus sp.* |
| **Little barley** | At each true rachis node **THREE separate ONE-FLORET spikelets**: central normally fertile, two lateral often reduced/sterile. Erect small head, annual base, tiny 0.2–0.8mm membrane ligule. | Not three lemma awns; winter plants may be vegetative and not identifiable yet |
| **Carolina canarygrass** | Dense spike-*like panicle*, not simple spike. Each ~4.2–5.6mm spikelet has **TWO tiny basal sterile lemmas plus ONE fertile upper floret**. Broadly winged flattened glume keel; true membrane ligule 1–6.5mm in local Williamson County description. | Other Phalaris may have different basal sterile lemmas; whole head alone insufficient |
| **Fern grass** | 1–8cm panicle with short stiff **three-angled branches**, with **3–10 AWNLESS fertile florets per 4–7mm spikelet**; ~1–3mm eciliate membrane ligule. | Old name *Desmazeria rigida* is synonym of accepted *Catapodium rigidum*, not extra species |
| **Annual beardgrass** | Fuzzy 2–15cm head owes its beard principally to LONG AWNS ON TWO NOTCHED GLUMES (~4–10mm), not foxtail sterile branchlets. Actual lemma ~1–1.5mm may carry a short awn too. Tall lacerate membrane ligule and annual damp-site crown. | Don't teach 'no lemma awn' as universal; don't call it a foxtail |
| **Cereal rye** | **ONE spikelet per ear node**, usually bearing TWO (sometimes three) FLORETS. Glumes with small ~1–3mm awns; **lemmas with longer 7–50mm awns**. Membrane ligule, short auricles. | Barley has three spikelets per node and Elymus pairs; annual cultivated rye may not shatter the rachis |

### Highest-priority visual roles (existing gallery QA FIRST)

1. **Same-scale rachis-node comparison:** one *Secale* spikelet with two florets, one *Hordeum* node holding three spikelets, one *Elymus* node holding a pair of multi-floret spikelets.
2. **Elymus paired-glume bases:** true V vs U insertion, annotated with glume origin and lemma awn, correct source specimen for both. Multiple nodes to avoid hybrids.
3. **Phalaris dissection:** both winged GLUMES and clearly marked TWO reduced sterile lemmas plus one fertile.
4. **Catapodium:** a genuine short three-angled branch and one measured 3–10-flowered spikelet. Don't confuse the branch with a flattened leaflet.
5. **Polypogon:** an actual awned glume with notched apex, separated from the much shorter possibly awned lemma, plus true 3–10mm inner membrane.
6. **Winter/early spring:** rooted little barley and rye annuals, and mature/dry wildrye spikelets attached to confirmed plants. Do not infer an annual or a species from a detached head.
