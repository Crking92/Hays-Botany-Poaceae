## Exact primary mapping

Angleton: frequently erect/prostrate and stoloniferous perennial; initially densely bearded culm nodes often hairless in age; hairless short sheaths; truncate erose hyaline ligule about 1 mm; blades 3–6 mm wide, hairless or sparsely hispid with papilla-based hairs. Inflorescence axis/branch bases pubescent; rachis/pedicels stiff-hairy, sometimes villous. Sessile spikelets 4–5 mm; first glume laterally keeled with slightly winged upper margins, typically broad/blunt apex, sparse marginal/basal often papilla-based hairs. Perfect-floret lemma awn brownish, twisted/weakly twice-geniculate, 15–25 mm. Do not transfer glume hairs to a blade, a first-glume apex to a lemma, or hair loss at a culm node to a flowering axis.

Kleberg: Gould explicitly supplies erect and stoloniferous culms, hairless inflorescence axis/branch bases and relative tendency to shorter spikelets, awns and branches than Angleton.
