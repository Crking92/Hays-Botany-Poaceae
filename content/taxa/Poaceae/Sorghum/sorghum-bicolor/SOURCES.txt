Sorghum bicolor (L.) Moench — sorghum — approved Master Plant Pool row 1110.
Accepted species in Poaceae, Sorghum: Plants of the World Online, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew: https://powo.science.kew.org/
LOOK HERE FIRST: robust erect grass, broad flat leaf blades with prominent midrib, and a terminal panicle of paired spikelets. Cultivars differ widely in compactness; do not rely on head shape alone. Need future macro showing sessile and pedicellate spikelets and leaf ligule.
