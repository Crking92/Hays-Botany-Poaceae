Chloris cucullata — Master Plant Pool row 258, reviewed 2026-10-10. Learner source-pool approval distinct from county-native certification.
Accepted POWO identity: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:395849-1
Existing approved Master Plant Pool diagnostic review: Inspect a mature spikelet under 10–20× first: upper sterile lemma is conspicuously hooded, swollen and nearly round (~1–1.5 mm), often awnless or with 0–1.5 mm awn. Measure the fertile lemma awn separately (~0.3–1.5 mm). Confirm crowded whorls of ~10–20 short racemes, typically 2–5 cm.
Look-alikes: C. verticillata has longer 5–15 cm racemes and much longer fertile (~4.8–9 mm) and sterile (~3.2–7 mm) awns, without the strongly inflated hood. C. virgata is typically an annual with one digitate terminal whorl and conspicuous long marginal lemma hairs. C. × subdolichostachya may be intermediate.
Target zone UNRESOLVED, not confirmed target native. C. × subdolichostachya is an accepted hybrid of C. cucullata × C. verticillata; preserve as distinct reference, do not collapse into parents.
