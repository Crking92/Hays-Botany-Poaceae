Chloris virgata Sw. — feather fingergrass. Approved Master Plant Pool row 260.
Accepted species: Kew Plants of the World Online, Chloris virgata Sw. Species-level concept distinct from C. cucullata and C. verticillata.
Look first: annual crown; terminal whorl of approximately 4–20 racemes; mature fertile lemma with long apical marginal hairs and an awn. Separate from perennial C. verticillata (multiple spaced whorls) and C. cucullata (inflated sterile lemma hood, short awns). Review full morphology and cautions in species.json.
Target-zone range: UNRESOLVED. Do not count as a confirmed target native.
