Echinochloa crus-galli (L.) P.Beauv. — accepted taxon: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/399660-1
Master Plant Pool row 402. Native-by-county review deferred; no target-zone native certification asserted.
Morphology: https://www.ontario.ca/document/weed-identification-guide-ontario-crops/barnyard-grass
LOOK HERE FIRST: At the leaf blade/sheath junction check for NO ligule and NO auricles; then inspect the coarse, branched panicle with crowded spikelets and often bristly branch axes. Awn length varies. The selected first image shows the seedhead only; collar/ligule needs a later diagnostic macro.
